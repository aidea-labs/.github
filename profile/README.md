## Welcome to AIdea-Labs 👋

[AIdea-Labs](https://aidea-labs.com) is a **research-driven** organization under Shanghai Bayes View Information Technology Co., Ltd. 
Our core objective is to empower research in philosophy and the social sciences through artificial intelligence technology.

Currently, we are working on the following:
- [Marx-Max](https://marx-max.aidea-labs.com): A unified, one-stop research workspace designed specifically for social science scholars.
- [Data Identity Protocol](https://dip.aidea-labs.com): A data center that we have collected, organized, and verified. (Keep going)
- [MCP-for-Stata](https://github.com/sepinetam/mcp-for-stata): A complete toolkit including MCP, CLI, Skill and SubAgent for enabling an agent to use Stata
- [Tabra](https://github.com/aidea-labs/tabra): A python toolkit for empirical research workflows, designed for data processing, model estimation, result export, and reproducible analysis.

If you are interested in what we do, please feel free to [contact us](mailto:sepine@aidea-labs.com).
